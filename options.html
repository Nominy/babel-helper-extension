Shortcuts

Record a replacement or add an alternative, then press the desired key combination. Escape and Tab can be assigned. To cancel, click Cancel recording or tap and release a modifier key alone. Clear disables an action; Reset restores its original shortcuts. Changes save automatically.

Shortcuts apply only where the action is available. Shared combinations may overlap across contexts; warnings do not prevent saving. Browser or operating-system shortcuts may not reach the extension.
